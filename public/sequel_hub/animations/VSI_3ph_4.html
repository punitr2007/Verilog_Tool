3-phase VSI (PWM)

A three-phase voltage source inverter is supplied from a $600\,$V DC source as shown in the figure below and is operated using sinusoidal pulse width modulation technique. The modulating signals for inverter legs a, b, c are, repectively, $m_a(t)=m_1\,\sin(100\,\pi \,t) + m_0(t)$, $m_b(t)=m_1\,\sin(100\,\pi \,t-2\,\pi/3) + m_0(t)$, $m_c(t)=m_1\,\sin(100\,\pi \,t-4\,\pi/3) + m_0(t)$, where $m_0(t)=m_2\,\sin(300\,\pi \,t)$. The switching frequency of the inverter is $2\,$kHz. The inverter is connected to a balanced star-connected series $RL$ load with $R=10\,\Omega$, $L=0.01\,$H.
  1. Determine the RMS values of the fundamental components of phase voltage and load current.
  2. Determine the amplitude of the 150-Hz component in phase voltage ($V_{an}$) and pole voltage ($V_{a0}$).
  3. What is the average current drawn from the DC supply?
  4. Which are the dominant harmonics present in the DC source current?

s_f2 = "150"

s_dt_nrml = "5e-6"

T = 1/f1
s_2T = ("%11.4E"%(2.0*T)).strip()

l = [
  ('$Vdcby2', s_Vdcby2),
  ('$L', s_L),
  ('$R', s_R),
  ('$f_carrier', s_f_carrier),
  ('$f1', s_f1),
  ('$f2', s_f2),
  ('$m1', s_m1),
  ('$m2', s_m2),
  ('$2T', s_2T),
  ('$dt_min', s_dt_min),
  ('$dt_nrml', s_dt_nrml)
]
calc.replace_strings_1("VSI_3ph_4_orig.in", "VSI_3ph_4.in", l)
print('VSI_3ph_4.in is ready for execution')
