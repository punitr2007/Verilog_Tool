Bi-directional converter

A single-phase bi-directional voltage source conveter is connected to an AC voltage source through an impedance $Z = R + j\,\omega\,L$ with $R=10\,\Omega$ and $L=0.1\,$H. The waveform of the voltage source $v_s$ is a square wave from $300\,$V to $-300\,$V. The phase and frequency of $v_s$ is the same as that of the output voltage $v_{BD}$ of the converter. The DC link voltage $V_{DC}=400\,$V. If the inverter frequency is $100\,$Hz, what is the average value of $i_{dc}$?
